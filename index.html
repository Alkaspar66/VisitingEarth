Memories from visiting planet Earth

In 2010 I left my home with nothing but camera and notebook. I didn't know then that this was the beginning of a journey that continues to this day. А journey that cost me everything I had, but which gave me something that money can't buy. The books I write, the music I play, the pictures I take - all these is my attempt to say "Thanks!" to uncounted amazing persons, places and moments that possible to experience only on beautiful planet called Earth.
